## Rating Policy And Scope

`Y` means most reviewed publications are reasonably relevant or adjacent to the ACM-grounded identity, even if individual records are misattributed or questionable.
`N` is reserved for really poor linked profiles, such as a wrong person or a publication list dominated by unrelated work; missing links also receive N by user instruction.
A confirmed erroneous paper is not by itself grounds for N.
Substantial contamination can warrant N despite a relevant majority, as explicitly decided by the user for the four cases below.
The judgment concerns the overall profile, not perfect attribution of every publication or the accuracy of aggregate citation metrics.

This revision reuses the 24,983 first-page entries in the September 16–17 UTC captures and the preceding ACM-grounded identity and topic assessment.
For 92 previously N profiles, a majority of sampled titles already matched the reviewed DBLP bibliography.
All 20 captured entries were individually reconsidered for each of the other 29 profiles against the ACM research context; their relevant/adjacent counts below are conservative lower bounds.
DBLP is corroboration only, and a topic match does not establish authorship.
The assessment covers the captured first pages, usually 20 entries, rather than entire bibliographies.
Scholar links, metrics, other roster fields and visualization data are unchanged.

The [all-Fellows row audit](acm_scholar_quality_2026-09-17.csv) records ratings as of this update, previous ratings, retained concerns, evidence links, capture metadata and reassessment grounds.
Its `review_requested` flag is separate from the quality rating: 25 `Y` profiles retain optional caveats, while the four user-rated `N` cases are resolved and missing-link `N` rows are coverage gaps.
`reviewed_at` records assessment time; `scholar_fetched_at` and `acm_fetched_at` record evidence capture times.
