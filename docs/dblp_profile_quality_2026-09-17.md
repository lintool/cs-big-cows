## Scope and Limitations

Reused all 1,653 accepted DBLP HTML captures from the September 17 Toronto crawl, fetched on September 17–18 UTC, and verified every capture hash.
Parsed 392,385 bibliography entries for corroboration and screening; this is not a claim that every publication was individually verified.
Individually triaged 490 profiles lacking strong corroboration from the reviewed Scholar sample, comparing ACM context with newest, middle and oldest entries and expanding flagged cases to broader title and metadata inspection.
A second lexical topic screen on the 1,163 profiles with at least five matches to a previously Y-rated Scholar sample produced 246 additional profiles for individual review; lexical disagreement was only a screening signal, never a rating rule.
Cross-service title agreement is corroboration, not independent proof of authorship; the ACM identity remains the reference.
Obvious incompleteness was checked against the ACM-recognized contribution and retained publication evidence, not solely a small DBLP count.
The row audit records inspection scope and evidence titles, including broader samples extracted for possible follow-up; not every extracted sample title was individually inspected.
The remaining supported profiles receive Y based on the retained identity review and corroborating publication evidence, without certifying their entire bibliography.
Records without an individual ACM profile use retained roster/directory context and are flagged as such.
The remaining flags are review requests; the seven explicitly rejected profiles below are resolved.
Counts refer to award rows unless labeled as distinct people or URLs; a recipient shared by both rosters can contribute two rows.
No replacement-profile search or new crawl was performed.

The [row audit](dblp_profile_quality_2026-09-17.csv) contains all 1,719 award rows, evidence metadata, reasons and flags.
Resolve its `dblp_capture_path` values from the `acm-bigcows` repository root; populated values begin with `../bigcows-crawler/.cache/` and refer to retained local evidence, not files included in a fresh clone.
`review_requested` identifies active review questions independently of `dblp_profile_quality`.
For removed links, `dblp_profile` is blank and `rejected_dblp_profile` preserves the rejected URL; the DBLP name, publication count, capture path, hash and `dblp_fetched_at` still describe that rejected page.
Those retained evidence fields must not be copied back into canonical profile URLs or crawl dates.
`reviewed_at` records the assessment and `resolved_at` records a subsequent resolution where present; neither is a crawl timestamp.
Snapshots, full extracted bibliographies, scripts, screens and validation are retained in `../bigcows-crawler/.cache/dblp-quality-2026-09-17-214051/` relative to the repository root.
