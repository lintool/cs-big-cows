Citation Timelines: ACM Fellows

About the Data
  • All citation statistics from Google Scholar.
  • Gray histograms indicate Google Scholar profiles of questionable data quality.
  • Darker bars precede the award year; lighter bars include the award year and all later years.
  • cites at award estimates citations before the award year: total citations minus captured annual citations from the award year onward, including the award year. As an example, for a 2023 award, we subtract citations in 2023 and every later captured year. This is approximate because histories may be incomplete and Google Scholar records can change; blank values mean the required data is unavailable.
